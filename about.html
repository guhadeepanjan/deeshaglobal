Founder

Shashwati Guha — Founder & Director

When I started Deesha Global, it wasn't to build just another advisory platform. It was to create something I had personally looked for — clarity in decision-making, honesty in guidance, and systems that respect both people and outcomes.

My journey has taken me across different worlds — engineering, education, global risk and investigations, and leadership development. Each phase shaped how I work: structure without rigidity, empathy without compromise, and governance without fear.

Many people struggle not because of a lack of ambition, but because advice is fragmented and decisions are made without a clear framework. At Deesha Global, we take a long-term view. We believe growth should be intentional, ethical, and sustainable. Whether we're guiding a student, supporting a career transition, enabling a founder journey, or using technology to improve outcomes, our approach stays consistent — clarity before speed, structure before scale, and trust above all else.
