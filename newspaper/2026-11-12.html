Cabinet extends Stand-Up India for five years, with a Rs 4,111 crore guarantee corpus

The Union Cabinet has approved continuation of the Stand-Up India scheme for five years till March 2031, with a credit guarantee corpus of Rs 4,111 crore and a target of supporting 2.5 lakh more women, SC and ST entrepreneurs. The Cabinet also extended the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units for five years, enabling collateral-free Mudra loans worth Rs 12.6 lakh crore over the next five years, and approved Nutrient Based Subsidy rates for phosphatic and potassic fertilisers for Rabi 2026-27.

RBI's Domestic Money Transfer Directions 2026 consolidate the rules in one document

The RBI's 11 November 2026 Directions consolidate domestic money transfer rules into a single document. The cash pay-out limit stays at Rs 10,000 per transaction, and the walk-in NEFT limit stays at Rs 50,000, CorpLawUpdates' summary of the official notification explains.

SEBI revises FATCA/CRS reporting under the Income-tax Act, 2025

SEBI's 11 November 2026 circular moves FATCA and CRS reporting to the Income-tax Act, 2025: section 508, Rules 238–240 and Form 166 replace the 1961-Act regime. Reporting financial institutions will need to rebuild their KYC software and reporting workflows to the new references, the regulatory summary notes.

Photo: SEBI Bhavan, Mumbai - Wikimedia CommonsRead at CorpLawUpdates (SEBI) ↗

NRIs and OCIs can complete SEBI KYC digitally from abroad from 3 December

SEBI's 4 November circular (HO/38/30/12(3)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25175/2026) lets individual NRIs, OCIs and foreign nationals in FATF-compliant countries complete KYC digitally from abroad from 3 December 2026. A separate 30 October circular stretches FPI KYC reviews to every 2 or 8 years from 1 December 2026.
