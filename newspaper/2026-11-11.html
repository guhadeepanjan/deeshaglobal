Farm fires, not firecrackers alone, behind the post-Diwali AQI spike: NASA scientist

A surge in stubble burning across Punjab, rather than Diwali firecrackers alone, is a major contributor to the sharp deterioration in air quality across northern India, says Hiren Jethva, an aerosol remote-sensing scientist associated with Morgan State University and NASA's Goddard Space Flight Centre. His calculations estimate around 34,000 fires across Punjab and Haryana between 1 October and 9 November; Punjab recorded 1,152 farm fires on Diwali, its highest single-day count this season, taking its cumulative total to 4,459. ISRO and CEEW research shows burning has shifted to late-afternoon hours satellites may miss.

Rocket Lab's twin ESCAPADE spacecraft fire their burns and head for Mars

The two Rocket Lab-built spacecraft for NASA and UC Berkeley's ESCAPADE mission - named Blue and Gold - have completed their trans-Mars injection burns, the company announced on 10 November. Each spacecraft swung past Earth and fired a single continuous burn of about 10 minutes, on 7 and 9 November, with greater than 99% overall accuracy. They are due to arrive at Mars in 2027 and begin orbit insertion in September 2027, to study the planet's magnetosphere.
