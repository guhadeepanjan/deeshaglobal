Retail inflation hits a two-year high of 6.09% in October, breaching the RBI's upper limit

India's retail inflation rose to 6.09% in October from 5.54% in September, breaching the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit for the first time since October 2024, official data released on 12 November showed. Food inflation spiked to 8.94% from 7.74%, with onion prices up 116.22%. The print is the last CPI reading before the RBI Monetary Policy Committee's 2-4 December meeting, and economists now expect another rate hike after October's 25-basis-point rise to 5.50%.

RBI's Domestic Money Transfer Directions 2026 consolidate the rules in one document

The RBI's 11 November 2026 Directions consolidate domestic money transfer rules into a single document. The cash pay-out limit stays at Rs 10,000 per transaction, and the walk-in NEFT limit stays at Rs 50,000, CorpLawUpdates' summary of the official notification explains.

SEBI revises FATCA/CRS reporting under the Income-tax Act, 2025

SEBI's 11 November 2026 circular moves FATCA and CRS reporting to the Income-tax Act, 2025: section 508, Rules 238-240 and Form 166 replace the 1961-Act regime. Reporting financial institutions will need to rebuild their KYC software and reporting workflows to the new references, the regulatory summary notes.

SEBI cuts the no-proof rupee derivatives limit to $5 million, matching the RBI's 95% tightening

SEBI's 5 November 2026 circular cut the size of rupee currency derivative positions that can be taken on exchanges without proving a real underlying exposure to a single limit of $5 million across all rupee pairs and exchanges, for domestic clients and FPIs. The change aligns exchanges with the RBI, which set the same figure on 10 October, replacing an earlier $100 million limit — a 95% cut.
