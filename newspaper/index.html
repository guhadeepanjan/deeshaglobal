Delhi overtakes Lahore as the world's most polluted city as air turns hazardous

Delhi overtook Lahore to become the world's most polluted major city on Friday, with air quality reaching hazardous levels and smog blanketing the capital, Reuters reports. Swiss group IQAir put Delhi's AQI at 334 (hazardous), while India's Central Pollution Control Board recorded 403 and rated it severe - the first severe reading of the season. The two figures use different scales and methods.

At least 23 killed as minibus and container truck collide head-on near Kaziranga in Assam

At least 23 people were killed and several injured when a container truck collided head-on with a minibus near Kaziranga National Park in Assam's Golaghat district on Friday, Reuters reports. Golaghat police superintendent Swapnaneel Deka told Reuters there were 23 confirmed deaths, seven people had been referred to hospital, and the toll was likely to rise. Police said the minibus was travelling at very high speed in a restricted stretch. Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma said he was deeply distressed and sent a senior minister to oversee relief.

New Zealand thrash India by 244 runs: Henry's 6/27, Young's 125 in record ODI rout

New Zealand beat India by 244 runs in the fourth ODI at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui on Friday - India's second-biggest ODI defeat by runs.
