SEBI cuts the no-proof rupee derivatives limit to $5 million, matching the RBI

SEBI's 5 November circular cut the size of rupee currency derivative positions that can be taken on exchanges without proving a real underlying exposure to a single limit of $5 million notional, outstanding at any point across all rupee pairs and exchanges, Wirony's explainer reports.
