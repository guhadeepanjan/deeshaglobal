AI that learns with light instead of electricity

Researchers at the University of Tokyo and NTT have simplified both the learning method and the network design to suit a device that calculates with light, matching the performance of complex neural networks with far less calculation. Sea near the Pacific garbage patch is slowly changing colour

University of Bergen researchers mining twenty years of satellite data (2002–2022) found the sea near the Great Pacific Garbage Patch now reflects more blue and green light together. Simulations point to invisible microplastics scattering light: the blue-green shift appeared in about 13% of modelled cases with microplastics added, against roughly 3% with natural changes alone. Direct checks in the ocean are still needed.

RBI brings payment operators under one cybersecurity rulebook

The Reserve Bank of India's consolidated Master Directions, issued 5 November, bring non-bank payment system operators — payment aggregators, card networks, prepaid instrument issuers and white-label ATM operators — under a single cybersecurity framework. Operators must hold source code or escrow arrangements for critical third-party software, undergo annual independent system audits, and keep the six-hour cyber-incident reporting rule. The framework applies immediately to large and medium operators; smaller ones have until 1 April 2028.

Photo: RBI, Mumbai — Wikimedia CommonsRead at Reserve Bank of India ↗
