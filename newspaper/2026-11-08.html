India and EU conclude talks to join the €93.5 billion Horizon Europe programme

Negotiations on India's association with Horizon Europe have concluded, the European Commission said. Once signed, Indian researchers and organisations can receive funding directly and lead Pillar II projects — health, digital, industry, space, climate, energy and the bioeconomy. Association is envisaged from January 2027, ahead of PM Modi's expected Brussels visit in mid-December for the India-EU trade deal.

ISS completes its solar upgrade: power rises from 160 to about 215 kW

NASA's 6 November update confirmed the station's eighth and final roll-out solar array is in place after a 6-hour 59-minute spacewalk on 5 November by NASA's Anil Menon and Canada's Joshua Kutryk. The upgrade lifts generation capacity by about 55 kW, closing a programme begun in February 2021.

Photo: International Space Station — Wikimedia CommonsRead at NASA via World of Now ↗

NASA's twin Mars probes swing past Earth for a nine-minute speed boost

The two ESCAPADE spacecraft, Red and Blue, will fire thrusters near Earth on 7 and 9 November, using gravity as a slingshot toward Mars. They will pass 965 to 1,770 km above the surface; each trans-Mars injection burn lasts about nine minutes. At Mars they will study how the solar wind stripped the planet's air.

RBI proposes tighter farm-loan checks and a new PSL calculation

The RBI's draft Priority Sector Lending Directions, 2026 would require banks to verify gold-backed farm loans against the district scale of finance, document land, crops and hypothecation, and keep alternative proof for landless cultivators. Trade bills discounted under letters of credit would stay in ANBC — every Rs 10,000 crore retained adds Rs 4,000 crore to the 40% PSL obligation. Comments close 26 November.

SEBI cuts the no-proof rupee derivatives limit to $5 million

SEBI's 5 November circular caps exchange-traded rupee derivative positions without proof of underlying exposure at $5 million outstanding, across all pairs and exchanges — matching the RBI's 10 October cut from $100 million, a 95% reduction. Positions above the limit need documented underlying exposure.

SEBI eases digital KYC for overseas investors from 3 December

NRIs, OCIs and foreign nationals in FATF-compliant jurisdictions will be able to complete KYC — and re-KYC — digitally from abroad from 3 December, without physical presence in India. Intermediaries may rely on central KYC records and video verification. FPI registration remains under the separate FPI framework.
