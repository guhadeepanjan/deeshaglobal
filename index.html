How verification works

Proof, not promises.

First

Official sources only

University websites. Government immigration pages. Employers' own sites. If it isn't published by the source itself, it doesn't go in.

Second

Date-stamped, always

Every record carries the source it came from and the date it was checked — so you can re-check it yourself in one click.

Third

Re-verified monthly

Rules change. Fees change. Routes close. The datasets are re-checked every month, and anything unverified is kept out — not guessed.

The Deesha principle
