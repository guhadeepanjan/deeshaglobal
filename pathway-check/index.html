Deesha Global · Relocation Advisory

The right country is the one with a route in for you.

A country can look ideal and still have no way in for you. Another, which you may not have considered, may hold a route you already qualify for. Our work is to separate the two, before you spend money finding out.

Tell us about yourself: your age, your education and work, your language, and the family who would move with you. We will assess your profile against every significant immigration route in force today across eight countries, 61 routes in all, and set out for each one where you stand: likely eligible, borderline, not yet eligible, or dependent on an employer offering you a role first. A country being attractive and a route being open to you are different questions, and we answer them separately.

Tell us about yourself

Eleven questions, a few minutes. Your answers are assessed here, in your browser, against the rules currently in force. Nothing is sent to us at this stage.

Please read this first. This assessment is general guidance, not immigration advice. It tests the answers you give against published official rules, as verified on 12 November 2026. It cannot weigh endorsements, skills assessments, employer decisions or discretionary judgements, and no assessment can guarantee an outcome. Before acting on anything here, confirm your position on the official source linked in each route review, or take advice from a licensed immigration adviser for your destination.

Which countries would you like us to examine in detail?
