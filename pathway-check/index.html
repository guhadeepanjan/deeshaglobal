Pathway Check

Don't just dream of a country. Check the route you actually qualify for.

A country can look perfect and still have no route in for you — while a country you never considered may have one you already qualify for. Pathway Check separates the two. Layer 1 ranks the countries worth considering for your profile. Layer 2 then tests 61 real immigration routes across 8 countries against your answers, one by one, and tells you plainly: likely eligible, borderline, not eligible yet — or honest about the fact that the route starts with an employer offer.

Built for Indian professionals first. Every rule, fee and threshold comes from official government sources — rules verified 2026-11-12 — and is re-verified monthly.

Please read: Pathway Check is general guidance, not immigration advice. It tests your answers against published official rules as verified on 2026-11-12; it cannot assess endorsements, skills assessments, employer decisions or discretionary judgements, and it never guarantees an outcome. Always confirm your own case on the official source linked on each route card, or with a licensed immigration adviser for your destination.
