How to read this

Three things parents should know

01

“Not published” is an answer

Where a university publishes no cut-off — for example, no IB total points — this tool says so plainly instead of guessing. A number you see on an aggregator site is often invented.

02

Subsidised ≠ scholarship

At Singapore’s six autonomous universities, the lower “subsidised” fee comes from the MOE Tuition Grant — a government subsidy with a 3-year work bond for international students. It is not a scholarship, and we never present it as one.

03

Scholarships rarely publish cut-offs

No Singapore university publishes a numeric entry minimum for its freshman scholarships. Selection is on outstanding results, leadership and co-curricular record — so be wary of anyone who quotes you a “scholarship cut-off”.
